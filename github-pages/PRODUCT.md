# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and engineering leaders evaluating agent orchestration, spec-driven delivery, and multi-harness coding workflows. They encounter Singularity as a guided technical showcase and need to understand both the system and the evidence behind it.

## Product Purpose

Present Singularity as a coherent control plane for coding agents. The deck should make its architecture, workflows, integrations, and provenance understandable while building confidence that the system is real, deliberate, and usable.

## Brand Personality

Tactical, cinematic, technically rigorous. The experience should feel like walking through a live command environment built from real system artifacts, with confident language and restrained spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages assembled from interchangeable card grids.
- Editorial-minimal layouts that soften or obscure the system's operational character.
- Abstract AI gradients, mascots, and unsupported capability claims.
- Decorative dashboards that look plausible but reveal no real workflow or evidence.

## Design Principles

- Turn architecture into a place the viewer can move through.
- Show real interfaces, artifacts, and sources instead of describing them abstractly.
- Keep every chapter focused on one legible idea within the larger narrative.
- Use motion and atmosphere to guide attention while preserving a complete flat fallback.
- Prefer technical specificity and visible provenance over marketing superlatives.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic headings and links, keyboard-visible focus, meaningful image alternatives, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and the non-WebGL flat experience. Aim for WCAG AA-compatible interaction and contrast patterns without claiming formal certification.
